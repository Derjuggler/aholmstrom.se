# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with static export (`output: 'export'`) and Tailwind CSS, deployed through GitHub Pages at `aholmstrom.se`. Pages are Markdown files in `content/`. The Obsidian garden was dropped in the October 2026 redesign and may return later.

## Users

The primary external audiences are potential collaborators and future consulting or speaking clients. Journalists are an important secondary audience. The site is also a durable public knowledge system for Anton himself.

Visitors should be able to understand Anton's expertise quickly, assess his academic credibility, explore his thinking, and either book a lecture or start a conversation about an assignment.

## Product Purpose

The site makes Anton directly bookable as a lecturer and advisor while grounding that offer in research on cyber resilience. It also publishes a curated digital garden from Obsidian and presents Dependency Mapper as Anton's own method and application.

Success means the site remains useful enough for Anton to maintain as a knowledge garden while giving an external visitor a clear reason and route to contact him.

## Positioning

Anton helps organizations understand information security beyond IT: how people, work tasks, systems, infrastructure, and organizational dependencies interact before, during, and after cyber incidents.

Dependency Mapper is the distinctive mechanism. It connects work tasks to underlying people, systems, and infrastructure, exposes bottlenecks, and supports scenarios such as “What happens if this dependency stops working?”

## Operating Context

- Anton writes and links Markdown notes in Obsidian.
- Only notes deliberately marked `publish: true` may leave the private vault.
- The public garden may contain Swedish and English notes in their original language.
- The Swedish site is the primary professional entrance and offer.
- English versions are required for profile, research, and publications.
- A simple top menu (with a disclosure menu on small screens) replaced the tree navigation in the October 2026 redesign.
- The first release excludes `slides.aholmstrom.se`; that will be a separate later project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Swedish-first homepage and commercial offer.
- English profile, research, and publication pages.
- Pages for lectures, pilot workshops, Dependency Mapper, research, publications, garden, about, and contact.
- Primary calls to action: “Boka en föreläsning” and “Diskutera ett uppdrag”.
- Warm contact page with a short form and a visible email fallback.
- Dependency Mapper may be presented publicly by name with images and a short demo video.
- No private note may be committed to the public repository merely because it is hidden from navigation.
- Contact email, form provider, headshot, Dependency Mapper images, and demo video are still open inputs.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Anton Holmström.
- Lead identity: bookable lecturer and advisor.
- Supporting identity: academic researcher with deep expertise.
- Voice: direct, intellectually generous, confident, plain-language, and free of cyber-security theatre.
- Visual character: calm single column, monospace headings, warm background, one yellow accent, light and dark mode.
- Explicit anti-reference: no black-and-neon “hacker” aesthetic.
- The site must not feel like a generic academic CV, generic consultancy landing page, or lightly reskinned documentation theme.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the help Anton can offer; let academic depth prove the offer.
2. Translate information security into the language of work, organizations, and consequences rather than treating it as an IT-only concern.
3. Demonstrate the method through real structure, research, and Dependency Mapper instead of relying on generic expertise claims.
4. Keep the garden genuinely useful for ongoing thinking while making the publishing boundary explicit and safe.
5. Make every path toward contact clear, warm, and appropriately direct.
