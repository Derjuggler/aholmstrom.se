# Information security is bigger than IT.

I help organisations understand what actually happens when technology fails — and build capacity across the people, practices and systems that must keep working together.

I am a doctoral researcher in information security, a lecturer and the creator of Dependency Mapper. My work focuses on cyber resilience from a socio-technical perspective.
